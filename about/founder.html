Dr. Siavash Ardekani

Founder & Technical Director, ETROYL

ETROYL was founded by Dr. Siavash Ardekani to connect scientific understanding, advanced engineering, and real-world deployment. With more than 25 years of experience across scientific research, engineering, and technical leadership, he has worked on complex technological systems spanning sensing, digital and embedded systems, signal processing, real-time imaging, mechatronics, and other demanding, high-reliability engineering environments.



Scientific Foundation

Dr. Ardekani holds a Ph.D. from Université catholique de Louvain, awarded in October 2013. His doctoral research focused on ground-penetrating radar forward and inverse modelling, with a minor specialization in near-field electromagnetics modelling.

His scientific work developed a foundation in electromagnetic phenomena, radar sensing, modelling, signal processing, and inverse problems — disciplines that continue to influence the scientific approach behind ETROYL.

He also carried out postdoctoral research at the Royal Military Academy of Belgium, further extending his experience in advanced scientific and engineering environments.



From Science to Engineering

ETROYL is built around the combination of scientific depth and practical engineering experience. Dr. Ardekani's professional work spans the development of complex electronic and computational systems, from architecture and implementation through integration, testing, and deployment.

  • Digital & FPGA Engineering: Design and implementation of high-performance digital architectures, FPGA-based systems, and real-time processing platforms.
  • Embedded & Real-Time Systems: Development of embedded hardware and software, system integration, drivers, and real-time processing pipelines.
  • Sensing & Signal Processing: Experience in radar sensing, electromagnetic modelling, digital signal processing, and complex sensing systems.
  • Imaging & Mechatronics: Engineering of real-time imaging and video systems, as well as complex electronic and mechatronic architectures.


Technical Leadership

Beyond individual technical development, Dr. Ardekani has led and coordinated complex engineering activities involving multidisciplinary teams and international stakeholders. His experience includes technical program management, system-level decision making, architecture definition, risk management, and coordination of R&D activities across international environments.

This combination of scientific depth, engineering practice, and technical leadership shapes how ETROYL approaches every challenge: understanding the underlying science, defining the right architecture, making sound engineering decisions, and carrying the resulting solution toward meaningful deployment.



The ETROYL Philosophy

Technology should not exist for its own sake. A strong engineering solution begins with understanding a problem at its scientific and technical foundations, continues through disciplined engineering, and ultimately proves its value in the real world.

This is the principle behind ETROYL: connecting scientific knowledge with engineering capability and carrying ideas from understanding to implementation and deployment.

Science. Engineering. Deployment. That is the ETROYL standard.
