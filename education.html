Education & Resources

Selected learning resources, technical references and visual material for engineers, students and technical teams working across FPGA design, embedded systems, signal processing, sensing and related engineering disciplines.

How This Differs from Insights

Knowledge you can learn from and reuse

Selected Insights show how ETROYL approaches difficult engineering problems: the reasoning, trade-offs and principles behind the work. Education & Resources is different. It is the place for structured explanations, practical references and reusable technical material.

The two belong to the same knowledge ecosystem, but they serve different purposes: Insights explain how we think; Education helps others learn and apply.
