Electromagnetic Modeling

Electromagnetic modelling for complex near-field systems

ETROYL develops modelling approaches that connect electromagnetic physics with computationally efficient engineering workflows, with particular expertise in near-field and GPR applications.

Engineering Focus

Physical fidelity without unnecessary computational cost

ETROYL works on forward modelling, near-field interactions, numerical methods and model-driven sensing concepts, translating electromagnetic understanding into useful engineering tools and measurement strategies.

  • Near-field electromagnetic interactions
  • Forward and numerical modelling
  • Computationally efficient engineering methods
  • Model-driven sensing and measurement strategies
  • Support for radar and GPR system development

Typical Engineering Outputs

From physical problem to usable model

Depending on the problem, outputs can include modelling formulations, numerical implementations, analysis of physical behaviour, computational methods, comparison with measurements and technical interpretation of the results.
