Signal Processing

Signal processing engineering for real-time sensing and imaging

Engineering Focus

From mathematical models to real-time information

ETROYL works across the algorithmic and implementation layers of signal processing, with emphasis on deterministic real-time systems and physically meaningful data.

  • Digital filtering, transforms and feature extraction
  • Detection, estimation and parameter extraction
  • Radar and sensing signal-processing chains
  • Image and video processing algorithms
  • Algorithm-to-FPGA and embedded implementation
  • Performance, latency and numerical optimization

Typical Engineering Outputs
